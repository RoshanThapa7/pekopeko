Meet the founder

Bikesh Maharjan

Bikesh Maharjan founded Peko Peko Eatery with a simple idea: Sunakothi deserves a place where great food meets a genuinely peaceful atmosphere. Not another rushed city spot , a neighbourhood eatery built for slow evenings, cold drinks, and plates worth sharing.

" I wanted a place where people come hungry and leave happy , far from Kathmandu noise, close to good food and good company. "

Come say hi to Bikesh and the team. Try the pizza and wings, stay for coffee or a Blue Lagoon, and make Peko Peko your escape.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked

Where is Peko Peko Eatery located?

We are in Sunakothi, Lalitpur, Nepal. Many guests reach us from Thecho as well , we are a short ride from the Thecho-Chapagaun area and a peaceful alternative to dining in central Kathmandu.

Is Peko Peko in Sunakothi or Thecho?

Our address is in Sunakothi. Because Sunakothi and Thecho are closely connected, people often search for restaurants in both areas , we welcome everyone from Sunakothi, Thecho, and across Lalitpur.

What is Peko Peko Eatery famous for?

We are known for pizza, hot chicken wings, momo, and keema noodles, plus drinks and a relaxed atmosphere away from Kathmandu traffic.

What are the best dishes to order?

Start with our hero dishes , pizza and hot chicken wings. For mains, our momo (steamed, fried, or jhol) and keema noodles are customer favourites. Check our full menu for drinks and sides.

Is Peko Peko good for a quiet meal outside Kathmandu?

Yes. Peko Peko is designed as a chill, casual restaurant , great food and drinks in a calmer setting, ideal when you want to escape the noise and chaos of the city.
